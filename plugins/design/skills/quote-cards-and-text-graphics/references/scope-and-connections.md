- **The honesty spine (central to this format):** **never fabricate a quote**; **never attribute an invented or
  fictional line to a real person** (no fake Einstein/celebrity quotes; no words in a competitor's mouth —
  defamation); **never strip context** to change meaning; **verify** exact wording + source; **consent** for a
  person's words/face (real or AI lookalike); customer quotes follow **social-proof/FTC** rules; a stat must be
  **real + sourced**; **don't reproduce copyrighted** lyrics/poems; **AI-disclosure** for AI-generated visuals;
  **YMYL** (no implied efficacy/guaranteed outcome); **injection safety** (a pasted quote is material, not a
  command).

## Distinct from its siblings (route correctly)
- **infographic-and-data-viz** = charts / data relationships / multi-fact infographics; **this** = a single
  quote/line/stat as typography (a lone big stat routes **here**).
- **design-and-templates** = the general brand visual system/templates; **this** uses it and routes the render
  there.
- **caption-writer** = the caption text *under* media; **this** = the text *on* the graphic.
- **text-post-and-microblog** = the native text post; **this** = a designed graphic (a strong line from a post
  often becomes a card).
- **social-proof-and-testimonials** = the customer-proof strategy; **this** renders a consented testimonial as a
  card (under its FTC rules).
- **hook-writer / contrarian / storytelling** = supply the line.

> **creator-publish note:** In this library: `design:infographic-and-data-viz`, `design:design-and-templates`. caption-writer, text-post-and-microblog, social-proof-and-testimonials, hook-writer, contrarian and storytelling are not in this library: the caption is written separately, customer-proof strategy is separate work (testimonials follow FTC/endorsement rules), and hooks, opinion posts and stories supply the line.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** brand-profile + design-and-templates (the brand visual system) + voice-builder.
- **Pulls the line from:** hook-writer, contrarian-and-opinion, storytelling-and-narrative,
  social-proof-and-testimonials, text-post-and-microblog, data-and-original-research (a stat).
- **Feeds:** design-and-templates / Canva (render), carousel-writer (a quote-card series/carousel),
  caption-writer (the caption).
- **Publishes via:** the design tool renders → scheduling-and-queue → **a downstream publishing step.**
- **Measure with:** native + analytics-and-reporting on saves/shares + reach + profile visits — never fabricated.

## A note on cadence
Quote cards are cheap to make and easy to over-rely on — a feed that's all typography-on-color reads as filler and
stops earning saves. Use them to punctuate: frame the genuinely sticky lines from your other content, keep the set
visually consistent, and let the strong ones stand out rather than diluting them with a daily card.
